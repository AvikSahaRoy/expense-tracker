App passcode

The passcode lives in APP_PASSCODE in your Apps Script, not in this app and not on this phone — so it is never sent to a browser and can't be read out of the page. Anyone who knows it can get in on any device.

To change it: edit APP_PASSCODE in pwa-sync-api.gs, save, and redeploy the web app. Every device keeps working offline until its next sync, then asks for the new passcode. Nothing queued is lost in the swap.

Wrong guesses are counted by the script: after 15 in ten minutes it stops answering. Run resetPasscodeAttempts in the editor if that ever catches you out.
